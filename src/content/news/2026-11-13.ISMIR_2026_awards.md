The <b>[27th International Society for Music Information Retrieval Conference (ISMIR 2026)](https://ismir2026.ismir.net/)</b> has just taken place, and we are pleased to announce that the following awards were given to C4DM members:

* The <b>Best student paper award</b> was given to [MAJEPPA: Morphing and Assessing in a Unified Piano Performance Space](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.11026) by Jinwen Zhou, Huan Zhang, Weixi Zhai, Jinhua Liang, Aidan Hogg, Simon Dixon

* The <b>Best paper award, honorable mention</b> was given to [LiveBand: Live Accompaniment Generation in the Audio Domain](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.03803) by Marco Pasini, Javier Nistal, Mathias R. Bjare, Stefan Lattner, George Fazekas

* Best reviewer awards were given to C4DM PhD students Ben Hayes and Yannis Vasilakis


Congratulations to all! You can explore C4DM's contributions for ISMIR 2026 at: [https://c4dm.eecs.qmul.ac.uk/news/2026-11-05.C4DM-at_ISMIR_2026/](https://c4dm.eecs.qmul.ac.uk/news/2026-11-05.C4DM-at_ISMIR_2026/)

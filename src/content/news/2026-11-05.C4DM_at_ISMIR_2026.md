The [Centre for Digital Music (C4DM) at Queen Mary University of London](https://c4dm.eecs.qmul.ac.uk/) will be present at the [27th International Society for Music Information Retrieval Conference (ISMIR 2026)](https://ismir2026.ismir.net/), held 8-12 November 2026 at NYU Abu Dhabi, UAE. The conference theme this year is Crossroads, reflecting the region's position at the intersection of East and West and foregrounding human-centric dimensions of music AI such as social impact, ethics, health and education. The event is hybrid, with both in-person and online participation.
C4DM members are involved across the scientific programme, tutorials, and conference organisation.

<br>In the <b>Scientific Programme</b>, the following papers are authored/co-authored by C4DM members:

* [Learning Jazz Pianist Style with Cross-Attention Conditioning](https://arxiv.org/abs/2504.05009) (Drew Edwards, Akira Maezawa, Simon Dixon)
* [Steering Dense Music Retrieval With Open-Vocabulary Concept Discovery](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.08757) (Julien Guinot, Alain Riou, Elio Quinton, György Fazekas)
* [LiveBand: Live Accompaniment Generation in the Audio Domain](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.03803) (Marco Pasini, Javier Nistal, Mathias R. Bjare, Stefan Lattner, György Fazekas)
* [MAJEPPA: Morphing and Assessing in a Unified Piano Performance Space](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.11026) (Jinwen Zhou, Huan Zhang, Weixi Zhai, Jinhua Liang, Aidan O. T. Hogg, Simon Dixon)
* [A First Benchmark and Metric for Evaluating Polyphony in Symbolic Music using Voice Independence](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1_oVefG_MiXo_Gh75T8n1Gf_t5J8dp2Ui/view) (Eleanor Row, György Fazekas)
* [Diff2Mix: Controllable Music Mixing via Diffusion Models and Differentiable Audio Effects](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.05442) (Yisu Zong, Jinjie Shi, Joshua Reiss)
* [AudioCodex: Streaming Autoencoders for Live Audio](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.05887) (Marco Pasini, Javier Nistal, Cyran Aouameur, Stefan Lattner, György Fazekas)
* [Equivariant Music Transformer](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.03920) (Zixun Guo, Simon Dixon)
* [Understanding Automatic Mixing: A Subtask-Oriented Analysis of Two-Stage Mixing System](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.02835) (Jinjie Shi, Wei Hua, Kunzhu Xie, Make Li, Yuchen Liu, Joshua Reiss)


C4DM PhD students Yinghao Ma and Weixiong Chen are co-presenting <b>tutorial T4</b>: Evaluating Music Foundation Models: From MIREX To Multimodal, Preference-Aware Benchmarking (with Junyan Jiang and Yizhi Li). The tutorial covers evaluation methodologies for music foundation models, from classical MIREX-style benchmarks through to multimodal and preference-aware approaches.

On the <b>organisational side</b>, C4DM member Christos Plachouras is serving as New to ISMIR Co-Chair for ISMIR 2026.

We look forward to seeing the music information retrieval community in Abu Dhabi and online!

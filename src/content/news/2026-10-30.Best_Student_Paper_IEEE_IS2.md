Hadeel Omar, currently studying for an _MSc Artifical Intelligence_, received a best student paper award together with her project supervisor, C4DM academic Johan Pauwels. ["A Comparison of Protocols for Server-Side Binaural Audio Rendering on the Web"](https://qmro.qmul.ac.uk/items/2bdf0105-5602-41d0-af7d-267a7150ff36) was presented at the [7th IEEE International Symposium on the Internet of Sounds](https://internetofsounds2026.ieee-internetofsounds.org/) from 28 to 30 October in Cannes, France. The paper is based on the work Hadeel performed for her final year project while studying for a _BEng Computer Systems Engineering_ in '25-'26.
